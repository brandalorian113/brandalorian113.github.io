Privacy Policy

Sentinel-Bot is a personal project used only by its owner to connect his own Google Nest devices to his own self-hosted Home Assistant server.

  • The app reads and controls Nest device data (such as temperature, humidity and thermostat settings) through the Google Smart Device Management API.
  • This data is stored only on the owner's Home Assistant server at home.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account settings or the Google Home app.
